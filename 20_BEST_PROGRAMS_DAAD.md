# 20 — Best-fit programs from the full DAAD database (Oct 16, 2026)

> **How this list was made:**
> - Pulled ~770 master's programs from the DAAD International Programmes database (English-only **and** English+German).
> - Narrowed them to 129 in electronics, embedded and medical topics, then read every program's DAAD page: admission rules, German level, English level, fees, deadlines.
> - Kept only programs where **you meet every requirement**:
>   - grade ≈ **2.28** within the cutoff
>   - a B.E. ECE is accepted
>   - **German ≤ A1**
>   - **no GRE**
>   - **no tuition fee**
>   - no fixed subject-credit rules like Bremerhaven's
> - ⚠️ Facts change yearly — **re-check each program page before paying any fee.**

## Why there are no German-taught programs here
German-taught master's programs (listed on hochschulkompass.de) need **B2–C1 German** (DSH-2 / TestDaF 4). Your plan reaches A1 in Jul 2027 and A2 in 2028, so none of them is realistic for 2028.
The bilingual programs that *do* accept A1 don't fit you:
- **THM Gießen Biomedical Engineering** (A1, grade 2.7, free) needs a biomedical or medical-engineering *bachelor's*, so ECE doesn't qualify.
- **TU Ilmenau BME** needs C1 German.
- **Duisburg-Essen Embedded Systems Engineering** needs B2 German.
