## THE THREE STRUCTURES

| Structure | One-time cost | Recurring/year | Liability | Rejection risk | Best for |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Sole Proprietorship** ✅ start here | **~₹0–5,000** | **~₹0** (just annual ITR; GST returns only if GST-registered) | Unlimited (personal) | **None meaningful** | Layer 1 — first clients, lowest cost |
| **LLP** | ~₹7,000–15,000 | ~₹10,000–15,000 (ROC Forms 8 & 11, ITR, DSC) | Limited | Name clash (minor refile) | 2+ partners, want limited liability |
| **Private Limited** | ~₹8,000–25,000 | ~₹15,000–40,000+ (mandatory auditor, ROC, board, ITR) | Limited | Name/doc issues (fixable) | Raising investment (L2/L3), max credibility |

### 👉 Recommendation: **start as a Sole Proprietorship in your father's name.**
- Near-zero cost, near-zero recurring, nothing to "reject."
- Take client payments into a **current account** in the business name.
- Add **Udyam/MSME registration** (FREE, online, instant — gives you an official MSME identity + scheme access).
- Add **GST only when you need it** (see below).
- **Upgrade to LLP or Pvt Ltd later** — only when revenue is real, you want limited liability, or you're raising money. Don't take on annual-compliance cost before the company can afford it.

---

## IS IT COMPLEX? CAN IT BE REJECTED?

- **Proprietorship:** not complex. A CA sets it up in a few days for a few thousand rupees; you can even DIY the Udyam + bank account. **Nothing to reject** — it's just doing business under a name.
- **LLP / Pvt Ltd:** more steps (DSC, DIN, name approval, MOA/AOA, incorporation) but **routine** with a CA. The only common "rejection" is a **name clash** at MCA → you just pick another name and refile. Document mismatches (PAN/Aadhaar/address proof) are the other cause → fixable resubmission. **Not a real barrier for a clean applicant.**

## RECURRING (PER-YEAR) COSTS

- **Proprietorship:** basically just your **income-tax return** (and GST returns *only if* GST-registered). **Minimal.**
- **LLP:** ~₹10–15k/yr (annual ROC filings + ITR + DSC renewal).
- **Pvt Ltd:** ~₹15–40k/yr (a **statutory auditor is mandatory** + ROC + board compliance + ITR) — the highest ongoing burden.

> This is the single biggest reason to **start as a proprietorship**: you avoid ₹10–40k/year of compliance until the company earns enough to shrug it off.

## ⚠️ ONE THING TO CHECK CAREFULLY — Father's employer

You chose to register in your father's name (good for keeping *you* bond-safe). But your **father works at Bharat Biotech**, a biotech company — so check two things:
1. **Does his employment allow him to be a proprietor/director of an outside business?** Many companies have moonlighting / outside-interest clauses. A proprietorship is usually less scrutinised than a directorship, but confirm.
2. **Conflict of interest — largely dissolved by the v2 pivot:** Layer 2 is now **healthcare software** (clinic/lab/telehealth tools), not bioprocess instruments — so the company no longer operates adjacent to his employer's field. Residual caution: still confirm his terms, and if the far-future hardware venture ever touches bioprocess, revisit then (it's a separate entity anyway — below).
   - **Alternative if father's terms are restrictive:** register in **your mother's name** instead (she's not employed → zero conflict), or in the sister's name once she's an adult. Mother-as-registrant also pairs well with the WE-Hub women-founder angle.

## SUGGESTED SEQUENCE (cheapest, safest)

1. **Now:** decide registrant (father, or mother if father's terms are restrictive).
2. **Now:** Udyam/MSME (free) + open a current account. Optionally engage a CA for ~₹3–5k to set up a clean proprietorship (any local CA or an online service like IndiaFilings/Vakilsearch works — no referral needed).
3. **Skip GST** until a client needs it or turnover approaches ₹20L.
4. **Upgrade to LLP/Pvt Ltd** only at the L2 stage or when raising investment — a CA converts it then.

Net: you can be **legally in business for well under ₹10k**, with almost no yearly cost. Registration is *not* the thing to worry about.
