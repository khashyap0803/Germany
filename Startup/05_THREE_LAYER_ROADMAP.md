## PHASE 0 — LAUNCH (Jul–Dec 2026)

**Goal:** prove the cash engine + lay the firmware foundation.
- **L1:** one offer → first clients via platforms (Fiverr/Upwork) + cold local + inbound. **Lean healthcare where possible** (clinics, diagnostic labs, dentists, pharmacies) — every healthcare client doubles as Layer-2 research.
- **Firmware (Track B):** C fundamentals via the existing Germany-roadmap weekly plans. Never stops.
- **Family:** proprietorship registered (father's/mother's name); sister onboards post-result; mother light ops; **father's first domain-advisor sessions** — list the realest operational pains he's seen in labs/pharma/clinics over his career.
- **Gate → P1:** 1–3 paying clients + revenue signal.

## PHASE 1 — SCALE + PRODUCT DISCOVERY (2027)

**Goal:** real income stream + find the ONE repeated healthcare pain worth productizing.
- **L1:** templates, retainers, stack ₹5–10k/mo clients. Target ₹25–50k/mo (stretch ₹50k–1L).
- **🔍 Product discovery (the quiet main event):** keep a **pain log** — every time a clinic/lab/doctor client asks for the same thing twice, write it down. By late 2027 you should see 1–2 pains repeated across clients. *That* is the Layer-2 product; you never have to guess.
- **Firmware:** STM32 (GPIO, UART, timers, ADC).
- **⭐ Bond ends (~mid/late 2027):** join formally. Germany decision point: defer further or hand to sister.
- **Gate → P2:** stable L1 revenue + one pain confirmed by ≥3 different clients saying they'd pay.

## PHASE 2 — PRODUCTIZE (2028)

**Goal:** first HealthTech software product with paying users.
- **Build the product** from the pain log: e.g. clinic ops/follow-up SaaS, lab report automation, diagnostic-workflow assistant — whatever the log proved. Sell it first to your **existing L1 clients** (warmest possible launch), then their referrals, then cold.
- **T2 unlocks** (~₹50k–1.5L): product build + first marketing. Software product = far cheaper than the old bioprocess-hardware plan.
- **L1 continues** as the cash floor. Sister (CSE/AI) co-builds product; (ECE) deepens embedded for P5.
- **Compliance note:** health-adjacent software handling patient data → follow India's DPDP Act basics from day one (consent, data minimisation); if a product ever *diagnoses*, it may cross into SaMD territory → that belongs in P5, not here.
- **Gate → P3:** 5–10 paying product users + retention (they stay after month 3).

## PHASE 3 — PRODUCT REVENUE + FIRST HIRE (2029–2030)

**Goal:** MRR grows; escape the success-trap.
- **Product:** push MRR ₹50k–1L. Product revenue scales without your hours — that's the whole point.
- **🪤 Hiring rule fires:** recurring ≥ ₹1L/mo for 3 months → first delivery hire → 30% founder time protected for product/R&D permanently.
- **Hardware Fund** (10% of profit) accumulating since it triggered. Track B → RTOS-level skill.
- **Gate → P4:** ₹1.5L/mo combined, ≥1 employee, processes documented (mother's SOPs).

## PHASE 4 — REPLACE FATHER'S INCOME (2030–2031) ⏰ HARD DEADLINE

**Goal:** the family is safe before the salary stops.
- **Target: ₹2–2.5L+/month net** (EMI ₹1L until ~2036 + living + sister + buffer).
- Real SME now: services + product + small team. This is the **primary win** — everything after is ambition, not survival.

## PHASE 5 — THE HARDWARE VENTURE (gates-based, 2029–2032+)

**Goal:** the mandatory hardware dream — as a second company that cannot hurt the first.
- **Launch conditions (all three):** main company ≥ ₹1.5L/mo stable · Hardware Fund seeded · Track B skill real (RTOS-level).
- **Structure:** separate entity (own registration, own P&L, clean liability).
- **Entry routes in order:** ① **SaMD** — software that *is* a medical device (your existing skill, lightest physical risk) → ② **partner/white-label** with an established device manufacturer (their trust + manufacturing, your tech) → ③ own devices (only after ①/② work). **Never start at own manufacturing** — that's the fortress lesson.
- **Neuro/neuroinformatics:** enters here as the far horizon (heavily software/ML — a CSE/AI sister is ideal for it).
- Funded by the Hardware Fund + (later) investors — by then you have a track record to raise on.
