## WHY THE DOWNSIDE FLOOR IS HIGH (the reason to proceed)

- **L1 stalls at ₹10–20k/mo** → permanent side income + portfolio + sales skill + higher employability.
- **Product flops** → you're out T2 (₹50k–1.5L max), still have the services income, the healthcare domain knowledge, and the pain log for attempt #2. Software products can fail cheaply and be retried; hardware couldn't.
- **Everything stalls** → you spent T1 (~₹10–50k), learned selling + firmware, and **the Germany path is intact and *stronger*** (portfolio projects + work income).
- **≥50% of the ₹10L stays untouched until G2** — it doubles as the family emergency buffer.

**Capped downside + skills that survive failure + Germany fallback intact = the bet stays asymmetric in your favour.**

## WHAT RAISES THE PERCENTAGES

1. **Outreach volume** — G1 is nearly a pure function of attempts.
2. **Retainers + templates** — G2 is won by recurring revenue and delivery speed.
3. **Healthcare-leaning client mix in L1** — every clinic/lab client is free Layer-2 research (feeds G4 directly).
4. **The pain log** — build only what ≥3 clients repeated. Kills the "guessed product" failure mode.
5. **Sister goes CSE/AI** — real build capacity for L1 *and* the product (+5–10% on G2/G4).
6. **Father's domain sessions** — his pain-knowledge substitutes for the network he doesn't have; it sharpens *what* you build.
7. **Sleep + 70% consistency** — protects every gate from the #1 killer.
8. **Honouring gates + the untouched 50%** — caps downside no matter what.

## WHAT KILLS THE PERCENTAGES

1. **Not sending the messages** (fear → silence). Kills G0; G0 kills everything.
2. **Burnout / broken sleep.**
3. **Perfectionism-as-procrastination** (endless polishing — your Golden Rules trap #12).
4. **Building an imagined product** instead of the pain log's product → G4 dies.
5. **AI-habit leaking into firmware** → G3 fails silently → hardware venture loses its foundation.
6. **Skipping gates with money** (spending T2 before a repeated pain exists).
7. **Sister overload → backlogs.**
8. **Hardware drift** — "later" becoming "never." Guarded by Track B + the 10% Fund; check it at every quarterly review.
