## 🛡️ CONTINGENCY PROTOCOLS

> **Added**: May 16, 2026 — plans fail when life happens. These protocols keep you on track.

### 1. Illness / Health Interruption
- **Mild (1-3 days)**: Do Anki from bed (15 min/day). Listen to German audio. No coding.
- **Severe (1+ week)**: Full rest. Resume at 50% intensity for 3 days before returning to full schedule.
- **Rule**: Never try to "catch up" lost days. Just resume the current week's topics.

### 2. Motivation Burnout / Can't Wake Up at 5 AM
- **Trigger**: Missing 3+ morning sessions in a week.
- **Protocol**: Take a **deload week** — reduce to 50% intensity (1 program/day, Anki only for German).
- **Root cause check**: Is the material too hard? (re-watch videos) Too boring? (switch to project work) Too exhausted? (prioritize sleep for 3 days)
- **Recovery**: After deload week, restart at normal pace. Do NOT add extra hours to compensate.

### 3. Work Emergency / Overtime Spike
- **If work goes to 8-9 PM for 1-2 weeks**: Morning block is sacred. Skip bed reading. Weekend power sessions carry the load.
- **If overtime becomes permanent**: This changes the entire plan — revisit `08_DECISION_ANALYSIS.md` Option B (leave for embedded startup).

### 4. Job Change (Leaving Unistring Early)
- **Before bond ends (~Jan 2028)**: Pay ₹3L penalty. Calculate: ₹3L ÷ remaining bond months ÷ 30 = daily cost of delay. If study time gained > cost, it's worth it.
- **After bond ends**: Resign immediately (3-month notice). Apply to embedded startups during notice.
- **Adjust timeline**: More study time available → can accelerate Phases or deepen projects.

### 5. Resource Unavailability
- **Udemy course gone**: Download all course videos NOW via Udemy app offline mode.
- **YouTube channel deleted**: Save key playlists to Watch Later. Download critical videos with `yt-dlp`.
- **Book PDF corrupt**: Keep backup copies on the 512GB SATA SSD.
- **Website down**: Use Wayback Machine (web.archive.org) for key pages.

### 6. Family Emergency
- **1-2 weeks disruption**: Same as illness protocol. Resume, don't catch up.
- **Extended (1+ month)**: Pause the roadmap. Add the lost time to Phase 6 buffer (Jan-Jul 2028 has 6+ months of flexibility).

### 7. IELTS Score Below Target
- **Score 6.0-6.4**: Retake in Dec 2027 or Jan 2028. Focus on weakest section.
- **Score below 6.0**: Major concern. Shift to intensive IELTS for 2 months. Delay Goethe B2 exam.
- **Backup**: Some universities accept 6.0 (RWU, Bremerhaven). Apply to those while retaking.

### 8. German Progress Slower Than Expected
- **Not at A2 by Oct 2026**: Increase weekend German to 4 hrs (reduce embedded by 2 hrs temporarily).
- **Not at B1 by Mar 2027**: Consider paid italki sessions earlier (budget ₹2K/month).
- **Not at B2 by exam time**: Take Goethe B1 instead. Apply to universities requiring B1 or English-only programs.
