## HOW TO SEND

1. **One email per university.** Never CC several universities together.
2. **Copy the subject line and the body exactly.** Everything is already filled in.
3. **Optional but useful:** attach one PDF containing your consolidated marks memo and your degree / provisional certificate.
4. **No reply after 14 days?** Reply to your own sent email (so the thread stays together) with: *"Dear Admissions Team, I am following up on my enquiry below. I would be grateful for any guidance. Kind regards, Khashyap Katarapu."* Send that follow-up **once** only.
5. **Never pay anyone** for an answer to these questions.
6. **Dortmund is already sent.** It went out with "[Winter 2028 / Summer 2028]" still in it. When they reply, just say you mean **Winter 2028**.

### What Dortmund's reply told us (Oct 5, 2026 — Ticket #202610058159)
- **Correct address is `service-ese@fh-dortmund.de`** (they replied from it). Use it for any future email, quoting the ticket number.

- **For Winter 2028 your window is Feb–Jun 2028.** Submit the Pre-Check in **February 2028**, which means **IELTS must be done before then.**

- Your eligibility question will effectively be answered **by the Pre-Check itself**, not by email.

Emails about the Pre-Check aren't answered before the portal opens.

### What Saarland's reply told us (Oct 5, 2026)

## WHAT I LEARNED WHILE CHECKING (worth knowing before you apply)

- **FH Dortmund:** non-EU applicants must complete a **pre-check** first; application window **1 April – 15 June**. Minimum grade 2.5 (you: 2.25 ✅). Requires software engineering, control engineering, signal processing and systems engineering in your degree, and at least 180 ECTS.
- **Bremerhaven:** minimum grade **2.3**, or **2.6 with 2 years' relevant experience after your bachelor's**. You're at 2.25, so you pass even without the bonus, but only just. Non-EU deadlines: **31 December** (summer), **30 June** (winter), via uni-assist.
- **Several programs need some German** (Bremerhaven A1, Chemnitz A1, Deggendorf A2). Getting a Goethe A1 certificate early widens your options.
- **English:** most need IELTS 6.0–6.5; **Freiburg and Saarland need C1 (IELTS 7.0)**. Aiming for 7.0 keeps every door open.
