## 3. HOW TO STATE THE BACKLOGS (exact and honest)

Use the same wording everywhere — application forms, CV notes, motivation letter:
> *"I did not pass 15 course examinations at the first attempt (14 credit-bearing courses and one non-credit course). I passed all of them subsequently, and my degree was awarded with First Class with Distinction (CGPA 7.87/10)."*

**Semester 5 — one honest sentence, then evidence.** Every theory subject was failed in Nov 2024 and all were cleared in May 2025 with **SGPA 8.77** (including **S in Embedded C** and A in Computer Architecture & Microprocessors). Write the real reason in one sentence (health, personal circumstances, or a lack of discipline that you fixed — whatever is true), then point to the recovery: SGPA 8.77 → 7.82 → 7.95 → **9.50** in the final semester, full-time engineering job, and your GitHub projects.

**Never hide anything.** uni-assist sees every memo. Darmstadt and Bremerhaven already said failed-then-passed exams don't count against you.

## 4. MOTIVATION LETTER (1–1.5 pages, customised per university)

1. **Hook + who you are** — B.E. ECE (CBIT, 2026, First Class with Distinction), embedded hardware engineer at a defence electronics company, building towards **embedded systems for medical devices**.
2. **Academic journey** — the backlog sentence above (2 lines max), then strengths: Embedded C (S), RTOS (A), Computer Architecture (A), Sensing Techniques & Sensor Systems (A), final-semester SGPA 9.50.
3. **Professional experience — describe it truthfully.** Your role is mostly system-level documentation, power/RF chain analysis, BOMs, integration and test supervision. Present that as *systems engineering exposure* — don't claim design or coding you didn't do; interviews will check.
4. **Self-built evidence** — Projects 1–3 with links (STM32 drivers, ECG monitor with on-chip DSP, custom PCB). This is where Darmstadt's "C/C++, 32-bit MCUs, systems engineering, hardware design" gets proven.
5. **Why this program** — name specific modules, labs or application areas (Bremerhaven: medical devices; Darmstadt: industry semester; Lübeck: Dräger and biomedical labs).
6. **Goal** — embedded engineer in medical devices in Germany; long-term, your own medical-device work.

**Process:** write the draft yourself → AI for grammar and clarity only (keep your voice) → one human proofread.
