## ⚠️ ONLY RECOGNISED CERTIFICATES COUNT
A Udemy (or any online-course) certificate is **not accepted** by universities or the visa office. Accepted exams:
- **Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1** / **Goethe-Zertifikat A2** — Goethe-Zentrum Hyderabad
- **telc Deutsch A1/A2** or **ÖSD** — also widely accepted
Check current dates and fees on the Goethe-Zentrum Hyderabad website when you book.
